Privacy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Short version: we keep what we need to run Wackometer, we don't sell your data, and the scoring runs in your browser.

What we store

  • An account cookie. Every visitor gets an anonymous account so streaks, votes and Pro work without signing up.
  • Your email, only if you sign in, to send sign-in links (through SendGrid) and keep your account on other devices.
  • What you choose to share: Wall posts, Daily W entries, Pass the Pen lines, votes, reactions and your leaderboard name.
  • Usage counts, like how many Wingman messages you've used today, and site-wide daily totals like visits, which kind of site sent the visit (for example "google" or "instagram") and how many share cards were saved. These counts aren't tied to you.

Analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity to see how people use the site (clicks, scrolling, and anonymized session replays and heatmaps) so we can fix what's confusing. Clarity sets cookies and collects device and browser details. Everything you type is masked, and we hide every area that shows your lyrics, so your words aren't recorded. Microsoft's privacy statement: privacy.microsoft.com. Cloudflare, our host, also counts requests to keep the site fast and secure.

What we don't store

Lyrics you only weigh, without posting, stay in your browser. They're scored on your device and never sent to us or to our analytics tools.

Wingman and Roast mode

When you message Wingman or ask for a roast, your message and the lyrics in your Weigh pad are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write the reply. We don't keep a copy of those conversations.

Payments

Stripe handles payments. We never see your card number. We store the Stripe customer ID so we know your plan.

Your choices

Take down your Wall posts anytime. Delete your account and everything tied to it from Squad → Account settings, or email hello@wackometer.com. To opt out of Clarity, block cookies for clarity.ms in your browser.

Kids

Wackometer isn't meant for children under 13.
