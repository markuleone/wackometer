Privacy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Short version: we keep what we need to run Wackometer, we don't sell your data, and the scoring runs in your browser.

What we store

  • An account cookie. Every visitor gets an anonymous account so streaks, votes and Pro work without signing up.
  • Your email, only if you sign in, to send sign-in links and keep your account on other devices.
  • What you choose to share: Wall posts, Daily W entries, Pass the Pen lines, votes, reactions and your leaderboard name.
  • Usage counts, like how many Wingman messages you've used today, and site-wide totals like how many share cards were saved. These counts aren't tied to you.

What we don't store

Lyrics you only weigh, without posting, stay in your browser. They're scored on your device and never sent to us.

Wingman and Roast mode

When you message Wingman or ask for a roast, your message and the lyrics in your Weigh pad are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write the reply. We don't keep a copy of those conversations.

Payments

Stripe handles payments. We never see your card number. We store the Stripe customer ID so we know your plan.

Your choices

Take down your Wall posts anytime. To delete your account and everything tied to it, email hello@wackometer.com from the address you signed in with.

Kids

Wackometer isn't meant for children under 13.
