Wicked lists

Made automatically from what you publish each month, plus one list per genre. Add a short intro in your voice; it shows at the top of the page and helps it rank. Lists with fewer than 3 songs stay out of Google until they fill up.

Artists claim their page from the “Are you …?” box on it. Check it's really them (their email, or a DM from their official account), then approve: their links go live on the page and they get an email.
