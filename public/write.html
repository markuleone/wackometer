Why it's wack


The meter reads six W-metrics from your words and blends them into one wack score from 0 to 100. Then the crowd on the Wall votes on posted lyrics. When the crowd keeps scoring higher or lower than the meter, that gap becomes the crowd tune and shifts every new score.
