How to rhyme “animation” without sounding wack

Easy to rhyme. 60+ perfect rhymes, which is a trap: the obvious ones are in a thousand songs already. Reach past the first few, or go for a two-syllable rhyme.

Skip the first rhyme that comes to mind. Everyone reaches for information or education. Rhymes a little further down the list, like administration, operation, organization, nation, application, location, still land clean but feel like you picked them on purpose.

Go longer. Ending a line on a word with more syllables, like association, administration, organization, investigation gives the rhyme more weight, especially at the end of a hook.

Bend it. A slant rhyme like taken, occasion, graham keeps the vowel and changes the ending, the trick most rappers use to keep a run of rhymes going.

Questions about rhyming animation

What rhymes with animation?

The most common perfect rhymes for animation are information, education, situation, association, population, station, administration, operation. For something less expected, try a slant rhyme like taken, occasion, graham.

What is a slant rhyme for animation?

Slant rhymes share the vowel sound of animation without matching it exactly: taken, occasion, graham, invasion, classification. They sound fresher than perfect rhymes because the listener doesn't see them coming.

How many syllables are in animation?

animation has 4 syllables, with the stress on the third. Rhymes land hardest when the stressed syllables line up.
