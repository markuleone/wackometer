Questions about rhyming aboard

What rhymes with aboard?

The most common perfect rhymes for aboard are board, record, lord, award, toward, scored, afford, ford. For something less expected, try a slant rhyme like for, or, your.

What is a slant rhyme for aboard?

Slant rhymes share the vowel sound of aboard without matching it exactly: for, or, your, all, more. They sound fresher than perfect rhymes because the listener doesn't see them coming.

How many syllables are in aboard?

aboard has 2 syllables, with the stress on the second. Rhymes land hardest when the stressed syllables line up.
