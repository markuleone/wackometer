How to rhyme “although” without sounding wack

Easy to rhyme. 60+ perfect rhymes, which is a trap: the obvious ones are in a thousand songs already. Reach past the first few, or go for a two-syllable rhyme.

Skip the first rhyme that comes to mind. Everyone reaches for so or no. Rhymes a little further down the list, like oh, ago, low, below, pro, grow, still land clean but feel like you picked them on purpose.

Go longer. Ending a line on a word with more syllables, like undergo, ufo, overflow gives the rhyme more weight, especially at the end of a hook.

Bend it. A slant rhyme like tomorrow, radio, photo keeps the vowel and changes the ending, the trick most rappers use to keep a run of rhymes going.

Questions about rhyming although

What rhymes with although?

The most common perfect rhymes for although are so, no, know, go, show, though, oh, ago. For something less expected, try a slant rhyme like tomorrow, radio, photo.

What is a slant rhyme for although?

Slant rhymes share the vowel sound of although without matching it exactly: tomorrow, radio, photo, studio, solo. They sound fresher than perfect rhymes because the listener doesn't see them coming.

How many syllables are in although?

although has 2 syllables, with the stress on the second. Rhymes land hardest when the stressed syllables line up.
