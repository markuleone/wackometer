How to rhyme “prayer” without sounding wack

Easy to rhyme. 60+ perfect rhymes, which is a trap: the obvious ones are in a thousand songs already. Reach past the first few, or go for a two-syllable rhyme.

Skip the first rhyme that comes to mind. Everyone reaches for their or there. Rhymes a little further down the list, like player, hair, fair, wear, square, aware, still land clean but feel like you picked them on purpose.

Go longer. Ending a line on a word with more syllables, like player, aware, mayor, prepare gives the rhyme more weight, especially at the end of a hook.

Bend it. A slant rhyme like get, well, said keeps the vowel and changes the ending, the trick most rappers use to keep a run of rhymes going.

Questions about rhyming prayer

What rhymes with prayer?

The most common perfect rhymes for prayer are their, there, where, care, air, share, player, hair. For something less expected, try a slant rhyme like get, well, said.

What is a slant rhyme for prayer?

Slant rhymes share the vowel sound of prayer without matching it exactly: get, well, said, best, help. They sound fresher than perfect rhymes because the listener doesn't see them coming.

How many syllables are in prayer?

prayer has 1 syllable. Rhymes land hardest when the stressed syllables line up.
