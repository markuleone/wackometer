How to rhyme “surrender” without sounding wack

Medium. 15 perfect rhymes to choose from: enough room to pick one that fits the meaning instead of one that just fits the sound.

Skip the first rhyme that comes to mind. Everyone reaches for gender or defender. Rhymes a little further down the list, like render, offender, lender, contender, slender, blender, still land clean but feel like you picked them on purpose.

Bend it. A slant rhyme like never, better, ever keeps the vowel and changes the ending, the trick most rappers use to keep a run of rhymes going.

Questions about rhyming surrender

What rhymes with surrender?

The most common perfect rhymes for surrender are gender, defender, tender, transgender, vendor, render, offender, lender. For something less expected, try a slant rhyme like never, better, ever.

What is a slant rhyme for surrender?

Slant rhymes share the vowel sound of surrender without matching it exactly: never, better, ever, however, together. They sound fresher than perfect rhymes because the listener doesn't see them coming.

How many syllables are in surrender?

surrender has 3 syllables, with the stress on the second. Rhymes land hardest when the stressed syllables line up.
