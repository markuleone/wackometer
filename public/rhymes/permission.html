How to rhyme “permission” without sounding wack

Easy to rhyme. 60+ perfect rhymes, which is a trap: the obvious ones are in a thousand songs already. Reach past the first few, or go for a two-syllable rhyme.

Skip the first rhyme that comes to mind. Everyone reaches for position or addition. Rhymes a little further down the list, like edition, definition, opposition, tradition, recognition, transition, still land clean but feel like you picked them on purpose.

Go longer. Ending a line on a word with more syllables, like competition, definition, opposition, recognition gives the rhyme more weight, especially at the end of a hook.

Bend it. A slant rhyme like women, system, children keeps the vowel and changes the ending, the trick most rappers use to keep a run of rhymes going.

Questions about rhyming permission

What rhymes with permission?

The most common perfect rhymes for permission are position, addition, commission, competition, mission, condition, edition, definition. For something less expected, try a slant rhyme like women, system, children.

What is a slant rhyme for permission?

Slant rhymes share the vowel sound of permission without matching it exactly: women, system, children, given, million. They sound fresher than perfect rhymes because the listener doesn't see them coming.

How many syllables are in permission?

permission has 3 syllables, with the stress on the second. Rhymes land hardest when the stressed syllables line up.
