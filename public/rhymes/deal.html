How to rhyme “deal” without sounding wack

Medium. 29 perfect rhymes to choose from: enough room to pick one that fits the meaning instead of one that just fits the sound.

Skip the first rhyme that comes to mind. Everyone reaches for feel or real. Rhymes a little further down the list, like ideal, reveal, steal, seal, heal, automobile, still land clean but feel like you picked them on purpose.

Go longer. Ending a line on a word with more syllables, like appeal, ideal, reveal, automobile gives the rhyme more weight, especially at the end of a hook.

Bend it. A slant rhyme like here, need, each keeps the vowel and changes the ending, the trick most rappers use to keep a run of rhymes going.

Questions about rhyming deal

What rhymes with deal?

The most common perfect rhymes for deal are feel, real, steel, appeal, wheel, meal, ideal, reveal. For something less expected, try a slant rhyme like here, need, each.

What is a slant rhyme for deal?

Slant rhymes share the vowel sound of deal without matching it exactly: here, need, each, keep, week. They sound fresher than perfect rhymes because the listener doesn't see them coming.

How many syllables are in deal?

deal has 1 syllable. Rhymes land hardest when the stressed syllables line up.
