How to rhyme “blast” without sounding wack

Medium. 17 perfect rhymes to choose from: enough room to pick one that fits the meaning instead of one that just fits the sound.

Skip the first rhyme that comes to mind. Everyone reaches for last or past. Rhymes a little further down the list, like cast, vast, contrast, surpassed, caste, harassed, still land clean but feel like you picked them on purpose.

Go longer. Ending a line on a word with more syllables, like contrast, surpassed, harassed, amassed gives the rhyme more weight, especially at the end of a hook.

Bend it. A slant rhyme like that, at, had keeps the vowel and changes the ending, the trick most rappers use to keep a run of rhymes going.

Questions about rhyming blast

What rhymes with blast?

The most common perfect rhymes for blast are last, past, asked, fast, passed, cast, vast, contrast. For something less expected, try a slant rhyme like that, at, had.

What is a slant rhyme for blast?

Slant rhymes share the vowel sound of blast without matching it exactly: that, at, had, back, bad. They sound fresher than perfect rhymes because the listener doesn't see them coming.

How many syllables are in blast?

blast has 1 syllable. Rhymes land hardest when the stressed syllables line up.
