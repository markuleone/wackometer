How to rhyme “seen” without sounding wack

Easy to rhyme. 54+ perfect rhymes, which is a trap: the obvious ones are in a thousand songs already. Reach past the first few, or go for a two-syllable rhyme.

Skip the first rhyme that comes to mind. Everyone reaches for between or mean. Rhymes a little further down the list, like queen, screen, marine, dean, routine, gene, still land clean but feel like you picked them on purpose.

Go longer. Ending a line on a word with more syllables, like between, machine, marine, routine gives the rhyme more weight, especially at the end of a hook.

Bend it. A slant rhyme like team, seem, dream keeps the vowel and changes the ending, the trick most rappers use to keep a run of rhymes going.

Questions about rhyming seen

What rhymes with seen?

The most common perfect rhymes for seen are between, mean, green, clean, scene, machine, queen, screen. For something less expected, try a slant rhyme like team, seem, dream.

What is a slant rhyme for seen?

Slant rhymes share the vowel sound of seen without matching it exactly: team, seem, dream, magazine, supreme. They sound fresher than perfect rhymes because the listener doesn't see them coming.

How many syllables are in seen?

seen has 1 syllable. Rhymes land hardest when the stressed syllables line up.
