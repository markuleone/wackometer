The Wackometer Book of Clichés

Burning desire

phrase · worn smooth from overuse

Heard so often your listener finishes it before you do, and that's the moment they stop listening.

Instead, try

  1. “a want like a fever at 3am”
  2. “an itch under the cast”
  3. “hunger with the windows down”

Starting points, not answers. The best swap is the one only you could write: a real place, a real object, a real moment.

How to fix any cliché

  1. Ask what the phrase is trying to say (heartbreak, freedom, devotion).
  2. Find one object or moment from your own life that shows it.
  3. Describe that thing plainly. Specific beats poetic.
