“shine bright”: the cliché, and what to write instead

“Shine bright” is on the Wackometer's list of worn-out phrases. Your listener has heard it so many times they finish it before you do, and that's the moment they stop listening.

Fresh swaps for “shine bright”

  • glow like a gas station at midnight
  • beam like a fresh phone screen
  • burn like a road flare

These are starting points. The best swap is the one only you could write: a real place, a real object, a real moment.

How to fix any cliché

  1. Ask what the phrase is trying to say (heartbreak, freedom, devotion).
  2. Find one object or moment from your own life that shows it.
  3. Describe that thing plainly. Specific beats poetic.
